What a credit really costs

$/credit is every dollar we spent generating that day, divided by the credits we charged that day. Both halves are deliberate: failed and free guides cost real money, and a price that ignores them isn't a price. Keep it under about $0.02 — the packs sell at roughly $0.04–0.05 a credit, so that gap is the margin. If a run of days sits above it, re-set credit_price() in a new migration; that function is the only thing that charges. Days before credits shipped show a cost with no credits beside it and read as “—”.
