Credit tools grant · take back

Granting mints an admin lot with your id on it, so the ledger can answer "who did this and why" forever. Take-backs are deliberately narrow: only a lot this console created, and only while completely untouched — a store purchase must be refunded at the store, and clawing back spent credits would put a traveller in debt for guides we already built.

Stuck holds credits in limbo

A hold is credits reserved when a guide starts building; it settles on delivery or is released on failure. One still held after three hours is stuck — the credits are gone from the balance and nothing was delivered. Release puts them back into the exact lots they came from; it does not rebuild anything.

Flagged photos a window — nothing here waits on you

Reports resolve themselves. A report hides the photo for the reporter at once and notifies the trip's owner, who can keep it or remove it — they know the people in it, you don't. A second report removes it instantly, and anything still reported after 24 hours is removed automatically. You never have to act here; each row shows how long it has left. This is still the only place in the console that shows a traveller's photo, and only reported ones ever appear — nothing can list an album.

The buttons work if you ever want to step in early: Remove deletes it for everyone now; Keep clears the report the way the owner would.

The scorecard by pipeline version

Every guide is scored at birth by ~40 deterministic checks — pure arithmetic, no model judging a model. The number to watch is clean % (guides with zero hard failures), and only within one pipeline version: the score is stamped by whichever rulebook existed that day, so comparing a pv1 guide against a pv44 one compares two different exams. A fix you ship today moves NOTHING here until new guides generate — the old rows are history, not a dashboard.
